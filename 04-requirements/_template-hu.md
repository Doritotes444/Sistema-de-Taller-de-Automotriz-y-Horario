# 04 — User Story Template

Use this standard template when documenting new functional requirements for the
Automotive Technical Support Management System.

## Header Information

- **Story ID:** HU-[XX]
- **Feature Area:** [Registry | Operations | Diagnosis | Warranties | Clinical]
- **Priority:** [Critical | High | Medium | Low]

## Story Definition

- **As a** [Administrator | Technician | Customer],
- **I want to** [action or capability desired],
- **So that** [business benefit or outcome achieved].

## Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then)

### Scenario 1: Successful Execution
- **Given** [precondition or initial state],
- **When** [actor performs action],
- **Then** [expected result and state change].

### Scenario 2: Business Rule Violation
- **Given** [precondition],
- **When** [invalid action is attempted],
- **Then** [system rejects with descriptive error code].
