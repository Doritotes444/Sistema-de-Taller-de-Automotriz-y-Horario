# 03 — Problem Framing

This document articulates the operational pain points of independent automotive workshops
that motivate the development of this platform.

## Traditional Workshop Failure Modes

1. **The Black Hole of Vehicle Status:**
   When customers call inquiring about their car, receptionists run around the shop
   asking technicians, wasting time and projecting amateurism.
2. **Untracked Parts & Phantom Repairs:**
   Mechanics replace sensors or filters from workshop storage without writing them
   on physical slips, leading to inventory shrinkage and unpaid repairs.
3. **Mechanic Overload & Bottlenecks:**
   Without systemic limits, the most capable mechanic gets assigned five vehicles
   simultaneously, stalling repairs while other mechanics remain idle.
4. **Unverifiable Warranty Claims:**
   Customers return months later claiming a part failed under warranty. Without
   an auditable repair history, the workshop absorbs wrongful repair costs.

## Value Proposition

The Automotive Technical Support Management System transforms workshop operations by:
- Enforcing real-time, digital work order tracking.
- Restricting mechanics to one active job at a time for focused craftsmanship.
- Creating an unalterable clinical timeline per vehicle that settles warranty claims instantly.
