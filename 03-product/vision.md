# 03 — Product Vision & Roadmap

## Product Vision

To become the operating system of choice for automotive mechanical workshops by
providing an intuitive, reliable, and auditable platform that turns every vehicle's
lifecycle into a trusted digital clinical history.

## Target Personas

- **Workshop Owner / Manager:** Demands complete visibility over shop throughput,
  mechanic workload balance, and elimination of unbilled labor.
- **Automotive Technician:** Wants clear diagnostic assignments, seamless logging of
  interventions, and protection against unfair blame for past vehicle issues.
- **Vehicle Owner:** Desires transparent communication, predictable repair times,
  and undeniable warranty records.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Turnaround Time:** 25% reduction in average hours from check-in to delivery.
- **Parts Capture Rate:** 100% of installed spare parts linked to billed interventions.
- **Warranty Resolution:** Instant verification of warranty status (< 5 seconds).
